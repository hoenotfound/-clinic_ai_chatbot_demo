One switch controls automatic temperature changes

Clear booking intent can move Warm to Hot immediately, explicit rejection can move it to Cold, and later AI review can update automatically managed temperatures.

How automatic temperature works

  • Clear booking or rejection intent can update immediately.
  • High-confidence AI scores may update automatically.
  • Medium and low confidence scores are recorded without changing the lead.
  • A staff-controlled temperature always wins.
  • Silence alone never makes a lead Cold.

Safe activation

Only eligible customer activity after automatic temperature is enabled can change Hot / Warm / Cold.
